About Us

Chop Chop is an African based resturant that provides various dishes. Not only African dishes but also continental dishes as well as Chinese and Italian dishes at very affordable prices. Our priority is the satisfaction of our customers.

The inspiration of this resturant came from a journey the CEO made to India. He had issues locating resturants that had nigerian food. Most of the resturants had only India's own local dishes. It was at that point he had the idea of having his own resturant in his home Nigera. He came up with the idea of a resturant that provided several dishes both continental and intercontinenteal so that travellers would be able to eat their local dishes here and feel at home.
